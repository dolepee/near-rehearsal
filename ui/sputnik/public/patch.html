One stranded bond

A Sputnik proposal failed with its 1 NEAR bond still in the contract. Can your change return it to the proposer exactly once?

Rehearsal builds your diff against Sputnik revision 5434a59 and checks it in a local Sandbox. An already-paid transfer must stay paid once, and a pending transfer must still pay its recipient.

This case covers sputnikdao2/src/proposals.rs. No model account or live DAO funds are used.
